Software for Redmont banks

Send RMD to a name, not a routing number.

RB Interbank is a free rail between member banks. You keep your Discord bot, your ledger, and your Treasury key. Inov8 only moves the message.

Join the rail Read the protocol

How a payment works

  1. 1
    Search

    Type a ticker, DRB:MDBZ, or a Minecraft name.

  2. 2
    Pick

    Every matching account at every member bank is listed. Click one.

  3. 3
    Settle

    The sending bank pays the other firm in-game. Credit happens after cash lands.
