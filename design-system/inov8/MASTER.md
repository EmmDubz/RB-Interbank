# Inov8 design system

Generated with [ui-ux-pro-max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill), then overridden to match the brief and the wordmark.

**Keep from the skill:** Trust & Authority + Conversion pattern. Minimalism / Swiss layout. IBM Plex Sans. 200–250ms hovers. No emoji icons. Visible focus. `prefers-reduced-motion`. Contrast ≥ 4.5:1.

**Override:** The generator returned dark navy, gold, and purple. Rejected — the brief is a **white / light-blue** site and the logo is a blue wordmark. Banking anti-pattern in the skill itself: no AI purple/pink gradients.
